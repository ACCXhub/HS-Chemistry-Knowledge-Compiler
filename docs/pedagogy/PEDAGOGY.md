# Canonical Pedagogical View Model

Status: **F1 canonical**

## 1. Purpose

Teaching structure is a projection over chemistry truth. It can organize, sequence, annotate, emphasize, simplify, and cross-link canonical records without changing their identity or ontology meaning.

## 2. TeachingView

`TeachingView` is identity-bearing because a curriculum/framework can have its own lifecycle, version, evidence, and navigation semantics.

A view embeds path/node values with view-local keys such as `D08/substances/inorganic/acids`. A path is a locator, not chemistry identity, and may change when pedagogy changes.

## 3. Framework coverage

The user's eleven framework diagrams remain pedagogical evidence and map naturally to linked views:

- D01 kinetics and equilibrium;
- D02 electrolyte solutions;
- D03 reaction types;
- D04 chemical calculations;
- D05 experiments;
- D06 notation and stoichiometry;
- D07 solutions and colloids;
- D08 classification of substances;
- D09 structure and periodicity;
- D10 elements and compounds;
- D11 organic compounds.

These are not ontology roots. A single canonical entity/reaction/fact may appear in many Dxx paths.

## 4. Macro / micro / symbolic projection

Teaching views may deliberately connect:

- macroscopic substance/material-system observations;
- microscopic species/structure explanations;
- symbolic formula/equation representations.

This is especially important for ionic solids, electrolyte solutions, precipitation, acid-base chemistry, and electrochemistry.

## 5. Derived teaching content

Views may include derived labels, summaries, prerequisite links, difficulty/order metadata, and notation projections. Derived teaching output must remain traceable to canonical source and compiler/rule provenance where applicable.

## 6. Constraints

Teaching views must not:

- mint replacement identity for chemical entities;
- convert a chapter path into a chemical class hierarchy;
- turn contextual textbook shorthand into unconditional chemistry truth;
- redefine a Reaction merely because a different equation form is taught.
